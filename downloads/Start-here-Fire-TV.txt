INSTALL STREAMBERT FROM YOUR PHONE

This guide is for Fire TV devices running Fire OS. For Android TV or
Google TV, open Start-here-Android-TV.txt in this kit. Both guides use
the same APK. No computer or USB cable is needed for the Fire OS steps.

CHECK YOUR FIRE TV FIRST

On the TV, open Settings > My Fire TV > About. Some devices call
this menu Device & Software. Check the device and software version.
If it says Vega OS, stop here. This Android installer will not work.
The Fire TV Stick 4K Select is one example of a Vega model.
Amazon model list: https://developer.amazon.com/docs/device-specs/identify-fire-tv-devices

1. GET THE TWO FILES READY ON YOUR PHONE

   • Open this ZIP in Files on iPhone or your file manager on Android
     and extract it. Keep Streambert-TV-Fire-OS.apk in that folder.
     If you downloaded just the APK, find it in Downloads. Don't
     open the APK on your phone. You will choose it in atvTools.
   • Install atvTools by tvDev on the PHONE. The iPhone version is a
     paid app. Check the price in the App Store before buying.

     iPhone: https://apps.apple.com/app/atvtools/id1661419573
     Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.vodik7.atvtools

2. LET YOUR PHONE CONNECT TO THE FIRE TV

   • Connect your phone and Fire TV to the same home network. A guest
     network may block the devices from seeing each other.
   • On the Fire TV, go to Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options.
     If Developer Options is missing, open About instead, highlight
     the Fire TV's device name, press the center button seven times,
     then go Back once.
   • Turn on ADB Debugging. If your Fire TV shows a general Apps from
     Unknown Sources switch, follow Amazon's instructions for enabling
     it during installation.

3. SEND AND INSTALL STREAMBERT

   • Open atvTools on your phone. On iPhone, tap Allow if it asks for
     Local Network access. Select your Fire TV from the device list.
   • If it doesn't appear, use the add-by-address option. The Fire TV
     address is shown under Settings > My Fire TV > About > Network.
     Type that number in atvTools. If it asks for a port, use 5555.
   • Look at the TV. When the debugging prompt appears, choose Allow
     while you are connecting from your own phone.
   • In atvTools, open Tools > Install. Select Streambert-TV-Fire-OS.apk
     from Files or Downloads. Wait for the success message.

4. OPEN THE APP ON THE TV

   • Find Streambert under Your Apps & Channels. If it doesn't show,
     open Settings > Applications > Manage Installed Applications >
     Streambert > Launch application.
   • On the first screen, scan the QR code with your phone camera.
     Follow the steps next to it to get your TMDB API Read Access
     Token. It lets Streambert load titles and artwork.
   • Tap the token field on the TV. In atvTools, use the phone
     keyboard to paste the token, then choose Save and continue
     on the TV. If the token is already in the TV's clipboard,
     use Paste from TV clipboard instead.
   • Open Start-here-TMDB.txt in this kit for the free personal API
     signup, or visit https://otosanx.github.io/streambert-tv-guide/#tmdb
   • Once the app opens, turn ADB Debugging back off. Streambert
     works without it. Turn it back on only when installing an update.

IF SOMETHING DOESN'T WORK

   • Phone can't see the TV: recheck the network, ADB Debugging, and
     the Allow prompt on the TV. Try entering the address manually.
   • Can't find the APK: extract the ZIP first and look in Files or
     Downloads. atvTools needs the .apk file, not the .zip.
   • Install reports a conflict with an existing copy: keep the
     existing app for now. An uninstall can erase settings and history.
   • Vega OS, Samsung Tizen, and LG webOS: this APK won't install on
     those systems. Use a separate compatible streaming device if one
     is available.

This kit has not been tested on a Fire TV in this session. The APK was
built and verified, and its phone transfer uses the documented atvTools
and Amazon methods. Player compatibility still needs a Fire OS test.
